# Lead Custom Fields

Lead Custom Fields let you track your own business details on every lead, such as "Expected Revenue" or "Product". They appear in the lead's detail panel. Open them from Settings > Lead custom fields.

## Field Types

- **Text:** Free-form notes, names, URLs
- **Number:** Revenue figures, deal sizes, quantities
- **Dropdown:** Fixed choices (e.g., Starter / Pro / Enterprise)
- **Date:** Contract dates, follow-up deadlines
- **Yes / No:** A simple flag (e.g., "Demo Completed?")

## Manage Custom Fields

### Add a Field

1. Open Settings > Lead custom fields.
2. Click Add field (or Add your first field).
3. Enter a field name.
4. Choose a field type.
5. For Dropdown, type each option and press Enter.
6. Turn on Required field if a lead can't be saved without a value.
7. Click Save field.

### Edit a Field

Click the pencil icon on a field to change its name, type, dropdown options, or Required setting.

### Delete a Field

Click the trash icon and confirm.

:::warning

Deleting a field also removes the values already saved for it on existing leads. This can't be undone.

:::

### Field Order

Fields show in the order you created them. To change the order, delete and re-create fields in the order you want.

## Examples

- Expected Revenue (Number)
- Product Interest (Dropdown: Starter, Pro, Enterprise)
- Industry (Text)
- Demo Completed (Yes / No)
- Contract Date (Date)
