# Lead Statuses

Lead Statuses are the stages in your sales pipeline. Open them from Settings > Lead statuses. The order here is the order shown in the pipeline view.

After calls, the AI sets a status for each contact based on the call history. Your team can set their own status too.

## Recommended Stages

If your pipeline is empty, CallVibe offers a Recommended pipeline stages panel with 6 ready-made stages:

- **0. New / Cold:** Raw inbound lead or cold prospect. No contact attempted yet.
- **1. Attempting Contact:** Outreach has started, but no two-way conversation yet.
- **2. Connected / Warm:** Active conversation in progress.
- **3. Qualified:** Customer expressed interest. Needs follow-up or a demo.
- **4. Nurturing:** Not ready to buy now. Check back in 3-6 months.
- **5. Unqualified / Dead:** Not a fit, for example no budget, wrong industry, or opted out.

All six are ticked by default. Untick the ones you don't want, then click **Add stages**. Stages you already have are marked as added. You can also dismiss the panel and build your own.

## Fields

- **Status name:** The label shown on contacts, for example "Qualified".
- **Colour:** A colour shown next to the name in the pipeline and contact list.
- **Position:** The order in the pipeline. Lower numbers come first.
- **Description:** Tells the AI when to use this status, so it classifies contacts accurately.

## AI and Human Status

Each contact has two statuses, shown side by side in the contact details:

- **AI:** Set by the AI from the call history. You can see why it chose that status.
- **Human:** Set by your team. Change it from the contact details.

## Manage Lead Statuses

### Add a Status

1. Open Settings > Lead statuses.
2. Click Add status.
3. Enter a name, pick a colour, and set the position.
4. Add a description to help the AI.
5. Click Create status.

### Edit a Status

Click the pencil icon next to a status, change the fields, and click Save changes.

### Reorder Statuses

Use the Move up and Move down buttons on a status, or drag it into place.

### Delete a Status

Click the trash icon and confirm. Contacts already in that stage keep the status. New contacts won't be assigned to it.
