# Settings

Settings is the configuration hub for your tenant. It's a set of cards, grouped in the order you should set them up. Each card opens one area of CallVibe.

## What You Can Access from Settings

**1. Get your data in**

- **Integrations:** Connect your phone system, WhatsApp, and CRMs (GHL, LeadSquared).
- **Manual Upload:** Add recordings by hand for calls your phone system never saw.

**2. What the AI extracts**

- **AI Insights Setup:** Set up call categories, extraction fields, and call scoring.

**3. Pipeline**

- **Lead Statuses:** Define pipeline stages for lead management.
- **Custom Fields:** Add fields (e.g., Expected Revenue, Product) to every lead.
- **Marketing Sources:** Manage lead-source labels.

**4. Account**

- **Preferences & Timezone:** Set your business timezone, targets, and opening hours.
- **Notifications:** Choose who gets alerts, through which channel.
- **Customer Management:** Manage customer records and how calls are matched to them.
- **Usage and Limits:** Monitor AI minutes used against your plan.

## Card Status

Some cards show a small status badge, for example Not connected, Receiving calls, or Timezone unconfirmed. Use it to see what needs attention.

The Integrations status comes from [Data Health](../integrations/overview.mdx#data-health).

:::tip

Confirm your timezone in Preferences first. Until then, CallVibe uses your browser's timezone.

:::

## Open Settings

1. Click Settings in the sidebar.
2. Click any card to open that section's configuration screen.

:::note

Settings is for owners. Anyone can open Preferences, but only owners can change it.

:::

Each section is documented on its own page in this guide.
