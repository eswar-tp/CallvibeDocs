# Account and Access

## Authentication Flows

CallVibe supports:

- Organization signup
- Email/password login
- Password reset and update
- Invitation-based team signup

## Signup and Approval

1. Create your organization account and enter your business details.
2. CallVibe checks your details. There are two outcomes:
   - **Account Created**: check your inbox for a verification link, then go to login.
   - **We're Reviewing Your Account**: the team verifies your business details and emails you, usually within 1-2 business days. Use Contact Us to skip the queue.
3. If you sign in while review is pending, you see Verification in Progress. The team may call for a quick 5-minute check of your use case. Your account is on hold until approval.
4. After the approval email arrives, sign in and start using CallVibe.

## Account States

- Trial: full access during the trial window.
- Trial expired: the app is replaced by an upgrade page until the plan is upgraded. Nothing is lost.
- Account paused: the workspace is switched off. Contact CallVibe to resume it.

## Roles

- Owner: Full tenant access, integrations, settings, team management
- Agent: Access limited to calls mapped to their agent names. Agents must have at least one agent name when invited

## Invite and Join Flow

1. Owner sends invite from Team & access.
2. Invitee opens join link.
3. Invitee sets full name and password.
4. Account is activated and can sign in.

## Password Recovery

1. User requests reset from Reset Password.
2. User receives email link.
3. User sets new password on Update Password page.

## Access Tips

- Keep at least one owner account active.
- Use agent-name mapping to enforce data visibility.
- If an agent sees "Account not linked" in the Data health pill or no data, map their agent names in Team & access.
- Review pending invites periodically.
