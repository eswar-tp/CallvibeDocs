# Outlook

Outlook is an AI summary of how your day, week, or month went across every call. Open it from Outlook in the sidebar.

## Choose What to View

Use the controls at the top of the page:

- **Daily, Weekly, or Monthly:** The period to summarize. A week runs Monday to Sunday. A month is the calendar month.
- **Arrows and date:** Move to the previous or next period, or pick a date.
- **Scope:** Choose Whole organisation, a team, or an agent.
- **Regenerate:** Rebuild the summary from the latest data.

Dates use your business timezone, set in Settings > Preferences.

:::note

Agents see only their own summary. Owners can see the whole organisation, teams, and agents.

:::

If there were no calls in the period, the page says "No calls this day".

## What You See

Outlook reads from top to bottom. The first screen tells you how the period went and what to chase.

1. **Headline:** A short verdict for the period, with key figures such as Calls, Negative, and Follow-ups. Weekly and monthly views also compare with the previous period.
2. **Rhythm (weekly and monthly):** Day by day activity, and patterns the AI found across the period. Click a day to open that day's summary.
3. **Customers:** What customers wanted, and who is still waiting on you.
4. **Execution:** What's working, and what needs attention. Weekly and monthly views show problems that kept coming up. Agent notes are included.
5. **Next steps:** Who picks things up.
6. **Where exactly:** More detail. Open Teams, Agents, Open issues, Flagged calls, Call topics, All metrics, or History.

## Generate a Summary

Summaries are written by the AI. If one isn't ready yet, owners can click Generate (or Summarise so far for a period still in progress). A summary for a period that's still running covers it up to now.

You can also get Outlook summaries by email. See [Notifications](../admin/notifications.md).

## Tips

- Start each day with the Daily view for yesterday.
- Use Weekly to spot problems that keep coming back.
- Click into Agents or Teams under Where exactly to see who needs help.
