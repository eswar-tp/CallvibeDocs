# Platform Navigation

The left sidebar is split into Main and Workspace.

## Main

- Overview: the landing page. Call rates against targets, volume, AI analysis coverage, and whether data is arriving
- Outlook: an AI summary of a day, week, or month, with patterns and what customers wanted. See [Outlook](./outlook.md)
- Calls: Search, filter, and inspect call records
- Intelligence: Call quality scores over time, how scores are distributed, and attainment by agent
- Follow-ups: Follow-up actions raised from calls. The sidebar badge shows the open count
- Contacts: Contact-centric history and context
- WhatsApp: Chat threads and message analytics. The sidebar badge shows unread messages
- Performance: Agent-level volume and quality. Agents see only their own numbers

## Workspace

Visible to owner role.

- Team & access: User invites, role management, and agent-name mappings
- Settings: Preferences, Integrations, AI Insights, Notifications, Customer Management, Lead Statuses, Lead Custom Fields, Marketing Sources, and Usage

## Top Bar

- Data health pill: shows whether calls are arriving. Select it to open Integrations. See [Data Health](../integrations/overview.mdx#data-health)
- Ask: opens the in-app assistant in a side panel
